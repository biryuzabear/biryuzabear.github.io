How the plate is drawn

The field on the home page is a seeded vector scene, not an image. A PRNG picks the shapes, the routing pass lays copper between them, and the whole thing is planned once and then repainted per frame as you move the pointer.

Cindersmith.createSigilField(canvas, (w) => ({
  seed, ink, highlight,
  satellites: w >= 900 ? 7 : 6,
  coreScale: 0.26,
}), { stage: heroStage, turns: () => 2 });

Planning is the expensive half — corridor checks reject any trace that would cross the core, a ring, or another net — so it runs on resize only. Everything else is a repaint on the next animation frame.

The scene is planned once and repainted cheaply, so pointer and scroll can move the layers at frame rate.
