Gravure title card generator

Thumbnail plate with a seeded alchemy circle, ring labels, and a duotone "engraving" filter for whatever detail render you drop in. Everything draws onto one canvas at full export resolution, so the PNG comes out flat.

Format

The dimmed bands are cut off in the grid. Numbers on the guides are the clearance from the crop edge to the title and subtitle — negative means the text is inside the cut.

Style

Ink drives every line, glyph and plate. A block with a plate flips to paper-on-ink, so the title and subtitle read as inverses.

Randomizing spins the hue wheel in OKLCh and keeps the tuned lightness and chroma ladder, so every roll is brass-teal in a different colour — same darkness, same punch, same ink-to-paper relationship.
